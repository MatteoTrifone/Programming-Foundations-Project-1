# Project idea: Time Tracker for workers and managers

**Problem:** Workers and their managers in small companies experience inaccurate and hard-to-check records of working hours in their everyday work, where start and end times are noted on paper or in simple spreadsheets, resulting in wrong hour totals, unclear overtime and extra administrative work for the manager at the end of each month.

**Goal:** Our program is a scaled-down time tracker inspired by Timebutler: workers clock in and out, every entry is checked for mistakes and saved to a file, and managers can see the hours and overtime of each worker without adding up timesheets by hand.
