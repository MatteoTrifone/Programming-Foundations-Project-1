# Project idea: Task Tracker

**Problem:** Students and part-time employees who switch between several tasks a day experience incomplete and inaccurate records of the time they spend on each task in their daily study and work routine, where start and end times are noted by hand or not at all, resulting in wrong timesheets, forgotten hours and extra administrative work at the end of the week.

**Goal:** Our Task Tracker makes time recording faster and more reliable by letting users enter a task, start and stop it, check each entry for mistakes and save it to a file, so that the time spent per task and per day is calculated automatically.
